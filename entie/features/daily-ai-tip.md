# Feature: Daily AI Tip

## What it is

A short, personalized AI tip at the top of the Dashboard. It suggests one small, concrete thing each partner can do to feel closer — a question to ask, a song to share, a way to show up today.

It's not a recap of the day. It's a gentle nudge toward action.

## How it works

- Each partner gets their own tip, written for them and addressed to them by their partner's name.
- The AI considers the couple's context: how long they've been together, the current Weekly Question, cycle data, and recent check-ins.
- A new tip is generated when the app requests it and the current one is older than 8 hours. Until then, the same tip stays on the card.
- Once a day at 8:15 AM (local time), if a fresh tip has been generated that the user hasn't seen yet, Entie sends a notification. If the user already opened the app earlier that morning, no notification is sent.
- Tips are suggestions, not directives. They're a starting point and may occasionally miss the mark.

## Who uses it

- **Both partners.** Each gets their own tip, written for them. Both can act on it.

## Why it matters

People want to show up well for their partner but don't always know where to start. The tip gives them one small, doable idea — no planning, no overthinking.

It turns *"what could I do for us today?"* from a blank page into a gentle nudge. And because each partner gets their own tip, both are invited to make a move — not just one of them.

## How to talk about it

### Lead with

- The small, concrete action — one idea, easy to try today.
- The personal touch — each tip is written for you and knows your relationship.
- Both partners take part — each gets a tip, so connection goes both ways.

### Avoid

- Sounding like a productivity dashboard or a to-do list
- "Insights" and "analytics" framing — keep it human
- Directive language ("you should", "you must") — tips are suggestions
- Promising perfect predictions or guaranteed results

### Sample lines

- *"One small idea for the two of you. Fresh a few times a day."*
- *"Not sure what to do tonight? Entie has a thought."*
- *"A tiny nudge toward each other — right on your Dashboard."*

## Edge cases worth knowing

- Tips are generated by AI and may occasionally miss the mark. Never frame them as authoritative — they're a starting point.
- Tips respect whatever life-stage mode is active (Cycle / Pregnancy / Menopause).
- With little logged data (e.g., a newly connected couple), tips may lean on the relationship stage and the Weekly Question rather than day-to-day context.
