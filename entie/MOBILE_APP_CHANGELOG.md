# Entie Mobile App Changelog

All notable changes to the Entie mobile app releases will be documented in this file.

This changelog is separate from [CHANGELOG.md](../CHANGELOG.md), which tracks changes to the Entie skill.

---

# App Store

## [2.1.0] — 2026-10-10

### Added
- Animated couple scene on the Dashboard. Two figures (man and woman, man and man, or woman and woman) walk along a path. How close they are depends on the Relationship Health Score: the higher the score, the closer they are. They might hold hands, walk side by side on the same path, or walk apart on separate paths.
- Her mood in the scene. When the woman picks a mood, it appears above the woman's figure. Both partners see it: on her Dashboard and on her partner's.
- New Growth tab with articles on different topics.

## [2.0.1] — 2026-09-14

### Changed
- Uread count messages bug was fixed.
- Medical consent: added website liks to privacy and medical data
- Added review card in Setting screen. It is appears when any of (Terms, Privacy, Medical) documents are changes.
- Trems, Privacy, and Medical links now opens in sheets and present a website content.
- Fixed a bug with Amplitude analytics: Now user id is changed with sha256 before sent any event to the analytics.

## [2.0.0] — 2026-08-27

### Changed
- Rewrote the mobile app from scratch.
- Introduced a completely new app design.
- Implemented the features documented in the Entie skill, with feature parity between the app and the skill.
